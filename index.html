About our project

Aerosols are particles in the atmosphere that come from sources like dust, wildfires, and pollution. We want to explore how aerosol concentrations have changed around the world over time.

What causes aerosols?

Dust

Dust from deserts can travel across continents and affect air quality.

Wildfires

Wildfires release smoke and particles into the atmosphere.

Pollution

Human activities can increase aerosol concentrations in some regions.
